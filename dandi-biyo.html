Privacy Policy

Dandi Biyo by Longest Chariot · Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Longest Chariot (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Dandi Biyo mobile game handles your information, including data processed to display advertisements.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

We do not require you to create an account, and Longest Chariot does not directly collect or store your personal information on our own servers. To save your gameplay experience, Dandi Biyo stores the following preferences locally on your device using Android DataStore:

  • Custom avatar name (optional, chosen by you)
  • Selected avatar, playing arena, and skill level
  • Campaign progress, stars earned, high scores, and longest hit records
  • Sound and haptic feedback preferences

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dandi Biyo uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements that help keep the game free to play. To serve, personalize, and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device and usage information, including:

  • Advertising ID (such as the Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Device type, operating system version, and app interaction/ad performance data (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Crash and diagnostic data related to ad delivery

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Opting Out of Personalized Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or disable ad personalization). You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

4. Children’s Privacy

Dandi Biyo does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at contact@longestchariot.com.

5. Deleting Your Local Data

You can delete all locally stored game data (profile name, scores, and progress) at any time by clearing the app’s storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling Dandi Biyo.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Dandi Biyo, please contact us at contact@longestchariot.com.
