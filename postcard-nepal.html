Privacy Policy

Postcard Nepal · Effective November 2026

1. Device-Only Data

Saved favorites, custom postcard drafts, and your theme preference are persisted strictly in local app storage. Postcard Nepal has no user accounts, cloud servers, or remote telemetry.

2. Photo Library Access

Read permission is requested only when you pick a custom photo in Create or Studio. Save permission writes rendered PNG postcards to the "Postcard Nepal" album with media location metadata disabled.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Postcard Nepal may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These partners may use device identifiers or cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies to serve and measure ads. Your personal postcard creations and favorites remain stored locally on your device.

4. Support & Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or need support with Postcard Nepal, please contact us at contact@longestchariot.com.
